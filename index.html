How to mod your LCD

  1. Design. Pick paper and watch model, click cells, add a photo or gradient. The preview overlays the real-time digits so you can check readability — busy, dark areas behind the digits make them hard to read. Light, low-contrast backgrounds work best.
  2. Export PDF. Every cell is placed at its real size in millimetres.
  3. Print at 100%. In the print dialog choose Actual size / Scale 100%, never “Fit to page”. Measure the 50 mm calibration ruler — if it isn’t 50 mm, your printer is scaling.
  4. Cut along the outline with a sharp craft knife and a metal ruler. Glossy photo paper or thin sticker paper gives the cleanest result.
  5. Open the watch. Remove the 4 caseback screws, lift the module, release the clips holding the LCD frame. Insert your print behind the LCD glass (the reflector sits there on the F-91W — the print replaces or sits on top of it).
  6. Test fit before closing. Trim a fraction of a millimetre at a time if it buckles.

About the sizes

Casio does not publish LCD insert dimensions. The defaults (F-91W: 23.0 × 14.0 mm, A158/A168: 25.0 × 15.0 mm) are approximations of the visible LCD window. Modules vary between production runs, so measure your own LCD glass with calipers and type the exact size into the Custom fields. Case references: F-91W 38.2 × 35.2 × 8.5 mm (module 593), A158 38.6 × 36.3 × 9.6 mm.

Tips

  • Print a test sheet with a few sizes (e.g. ±0.5 mm) and keep the best fit.
  • The polarizer darkens everything slightly: brighten your images by 10–20%.
  • Opening a watch voids water resistance unless you reseal the gasket properly.
