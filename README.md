# Casio Modder

Design custom LCD backgrounds for the **Casio F-91W** and **A158 / A168**, preview them with live LCD digits and in 3D, and export a **real-size PDF** to print, cut and install.

**Live site:** https://leonardo16am.github.io/casio_modder/

- A4 / Letter / A5 / A3 / Legal sheets, portrait or landscape
- Grid of real-size cells (F-91W ≈ 23.0 × 14.0 mm, A158/A168 ≈ 25.0 × 15.0 mm, or custom)
- Photo (zoom, pan, rotate, flip, brightness/contrast/saturation), gradient or solid fill per cell
- Live 7-segment digit overlay to check readability
- PDF in millimetres with cut lines, crop marks and a 50 mm calibration ruler — print at **100% / Actual size**
- Procedural Three.js 3D preview of both watches

Sizes are approximations (Casio publishes none) — measure your LCD with calipers and use the Custom size.
